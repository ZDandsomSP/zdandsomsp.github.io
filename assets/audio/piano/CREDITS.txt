Salamander Grand Piano V2
Author: Alexander Holm
Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Sample distribution: Tone.js audio collection (audio assets only; Tone.js code is not used).
Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/efd8296360f9526e379bfbe5c1698ff54d6a1d34/salamander

The 21 MP3 samples from C2 to C7 are unchanged, encoded as Base64 in a local
JavaScript data file to support both HTTP hosting and direct file opening.
Playback resamples adjacent notes by at most one semitone and applies a short
attack/release envelope and gentle peak compression.
Original sample information and attribution are retained in ORIGINAL-README.txt.
The sample license applies to the recordings, separately from the site's code.
No endorsement by the original author is implied.
